Matrix Massage & Bodywork partners with local gyms, fitness studios, chiropractic offices, trainers, and wellness providers who want a trusted local resource for clients needing massage therapy, recovery support, and a more complete bodywork experience.

​

Located in Salem, WI, Matrix serves clients throughout Kenosha County, Paddock Lake, Bristol, Union Grove, Twin Lakes, Trevor, Pleasant Prairie, Kenosha, and nearby Northern Illinois.

​

Our goal is simple: make it easy for local wellness professionals to refer clients who need focused bodywork, recovery support, and a high-quality clinical-luxury experience.
