# Page-type audit and new-page requirements

Mark every applicable check with evidence and status. “Required” means necessary for the stated function or a confirmed technical defect; it does not mean every SEO recommendation is a Google ranking requirement. An already satisfied check needs no edit.

## All indexable public pages

1. **Discovery and indexation:** correct HTTP result and redirect destination; intended canonical; no accidental meta/X-Robots noindex or snippet restriction; robots/CDN access; sitemap entry; crawlable incoming links. Inspect Google's selected canonical and index status when accessible. A `site:` search, sitemap listing, HTTP 200 or “live test passed” alone is not proof of indexing.
2. **Intent and metadata:** a distinct useful purpose, accurate title and description, clear H1 and logical headings. One main H1 is the Matrix convention, not a magic ranking formula. Avoid duplicated titles, unsupported superlatives, excessive city lists and rigid character/word-count rules. Metadata must accurately describe the page; do not replace good existing titles without a reason.
3. **Readable content:** useful main text available to crawlers, not image-only or dependent on an inaccessible iframe. Check Wix custom-element `seoMarkup` parity, semantic anchors, accordions and mobile content. SEO markup must represent the real visible page; never stuff hidden keywords or different promises into crawler-only text. Check actual response headers as well as HTML when available.
4. **Business identity and trust:** accurate business name, real Salem address, phone, service/location relationships and working contact/booking links. Appropriate scope and evidence for wellness/clinical claims. Use genuine experience, original service details and real photographs. Preserve authentic reviews verbatim. Link relevant qualifications/About/policies rather than inventing authors or credentials.
5. **Internal links:** map incoming and outgoing ordinary `a href` links, descriptive natural anchor text, main service hub to specific services/regions and useful return links. Confirm destinations, redirects and broken links. Sitewide footer links may already satisfy discovery; adding duplicate links is not automatically useful. Include any other pages requiring edits as separate approval IDs.
6. **User experience:** readable mobile layout, no overflow/clipped content or footer overlap, keyboard-accessible links/buttons/accordions, useful alt text on meaningful images and empty alt on decorative images, appropriately sized assets and loading behavior. Avoid lazy-loading the key hero image without evaluating performance. Validate map/booking links without making an appointment or purchase.
7. **Structured data:** inventory existing site-level and page-level JSON-LD first. Use schema.org types appropriate to visible facts. A consistent LocalBusiness/HealthAndBeautyBusiness entity may be linked by stable `@id`; reuse rather than create competing businesses. Verify name, address, phone, actual coordinates and verified sameAs URLs. Service markup can reference the real provider and relevant service area. Check parse validity, consistency and applicable Rich Results requirements; a Schema.org-valid Service is not a guaranteed Google rich result. JSON-LD in a supported head or body location may be valid; don't declare body markup invalid solely for its placement. Prefer the existing Wix supported structured-data mechanism and avoid duplicate injections.
8. **Conversion measurement:** relevant CTA, correct verified Square destination, accessible phone/text links, and existing event tracking. Verify consent behavior and duplicate tags read-only. Do not add new tracking tags, events or permissions without a specific proposal and approval.

## Requirements by type

| Page type | Useful content and links | Conditional additions / cautions |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage / main service hub | Clear business/service overview, Salem identity, distinct service categories, links to detailed service pages, booking and genuine trust evidence | Don't turn a hub into duplicate copies of every service page. Preserve original shared shell. |
| Individual service / pathway | What the service is, who it suits, real experience, duration/pricing when verified, expected outcomes without guarantees, relevant preparation/FAQ, booking, parent hub and related services | Service schema with real provider; offer data only when current and visible. Don't invent contraindications or make medical promises. |
| Geographic landing page | Service near target community, explicit real Salem studio location, useful local travel context, relevant offerings, unique local questions, map/directions when useful, main service and related regional links | Verify distance/time/route claims; label estimates. No fake offices, fabricated landmarks, “traffic-light-free” claims without evidence, city-name swaps or mass doorway pages. Map iframe is useful UX, not mandatory for ranking. |
| Booking page | Clear online booking path/working embed plus fallback link, verified practical policies and links to service information | Preserve conversion events, booking app and cross-domain behavior. Don't submit test appointments. |
| Gift card / promotion | Verified gift-card destination, what recipient can use it for, real amounts/terms/expiry and seasonal relevance | No invented discount or expired offer. Offers must match current actual availability. |
| Educational content | Accurate directly answered question, relevant first-hand knowledge, dates when material, appropriate authoritative citations and natural related-service links | Distinguish information from medical advice; no fabricated studies, authors or results. Update when substance changes, not merely to fake freshness. |

## AI search, local search and overlap

- Make factual answers easy to extract through clear headings, concise direct descriptions, consistent entity details and useful visible FAQs. These also improve human comprehension.
- There is no universal “AI schema,” compulsory `llms.txt`, fixed FAQ count, artificial keyword density, or guaranteed AI recommendation. Check current official guidance before claiming a new requirement.
- Google Search access, OAI-SearchBot access, user-triggered retrieval and training crawlers have different purposes. Do not change training consent or `robots.txt` globally as an incidental SEO edit. Any such change needs a specific scoped proposal.
- Do not guarantee FAQ rich results for this business. Preserve valid matching existing FAQ schema where appropriate; visible helpful FAQs do not require adding that schema everywhere. Never invent ratings or mark self-serving testimonials as eligible star results.
- Assess overlapping pages by intent, query evidence, inbound links and useful differences. Shared keywords alone do not prove cannibalization. Merges, deletions, redirects and canonical changes require a separately reasoned and specifically approved plan.
- Check Google Business Profile consistency and link destinations when authorized and accessible. Website work alone cannot control local pack distance/relevance/prominence, and a regional page does not make the business physically located in that town. Profile edits, review outreach or directory changes are separate approval scope.
- Manual AI-answer spot checks are labeled samples with engine, exact prompt, date and location/context. An assistant's generated answer about its own audit is not an independent visibility test. Referral traffic measures visits, not all AI citations or impressions; lack of referrals does not prove lack of mentions.
