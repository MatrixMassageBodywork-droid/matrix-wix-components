# Approved Matrix design system

Use the published M4 and facial/pathway work as visual references. Keep the website's native shared header/footer and genuine review section. Design a complete page body; never substitute a few decorative images for an entire page proof.

## Layout and typography

- Clinical luxury: cream `#f7f1e8`, near-white `#fdfcf9`, deep teal ink `#123d40`, dark teal `#102e30`, turquoise/hover `#087776`, restrained pale aqua `#eaf5f1`.
- Full-width backgrounds and hero image/headline panels, without black side gutters. Text can retain a max width for readability. Native Wix stretch must also be on; CSS width 100% cannot escape a 980px host.
- Subpage hero: cream text panel and service-specific image; compact approved logo, small eyebrow, one clear H1, outcome-focused lead and book CTA. Main hubs can use a centered logo over water.
- Approved subpage font pair: Cormorant-style serif headings and Montserrat body, with Georgia/Arial fallbacks. Use externally served, pinned font assets instead of embedding large base64 fonts in production renderers.
- Generous spacing; readable short paragraphs; substantial cream surfaces; teal/water used selectively. No all-teal wall, cramped three-column copy, giant dead bands or duplicate local footer.
- Responsive stacking with readable type and tap targets. Retain visible focus states, reduced-motion support, and image alt text. Full-width does not mean stretching photos out of proportion.

## Image selection

Inspect images actually used on adjacent pages before choosing the new hero and supporting image. Distinct photos are the default. Do not repeat the same stone/linen still lifes on every massage subpage. Reusing the logo and water is intentional; recycling service hero photos is not.

Prefer available verified real Matrix images when appropriate. The approved MLD release uses illustrative gentle forearm contact and a calm cream treatment setting. Do not show forceful kneading, deep compression, gadgets or dramatic muscle work for a light-touch service. Maintain relaxation and luxury without making unsupported clinical claims. Other services need their own accurate visual direction.

If new raster imagery is required, use image generation and inspect the output before integration. Avoid text/logos inside generated treatment photographs, identifiable invented staff, fabricated before/after results or presenting a generated room as the real studio. Preserve imagery provenance and meaningful alt text. Keep decorative water alt empty. Optimize display assets, keep originals and mappings in GitHub, and verify the exact uploaded Wix media URLs. Never show only image assets when the requested deliverable is a full-page design.

Suggested body flow, adapted to the service: hero → what it is / who it suits → benefits or reasons to book → what to expect with a different image → relevant suitability guidance → FAQs → related services → dark water booking close. Use page-specific content rather than blindly cloning MLD sections or medical screening text.
