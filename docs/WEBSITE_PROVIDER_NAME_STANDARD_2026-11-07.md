Effective November 24, 2026. The owner reaffirmed the existing instruction: all customer-facing copy must be MATRIX-neutral and must not promote an individual provider by name.

This applies to website pages, landing pages, offers, Black Friday campaigns, gift cards, blogs, FAQs, headings, buttons, image text/alt/captions, title/meta/social fields, structured data, crawler-facing markup, public Square service descriptions and booking presentation, and editable customer-message templates. The visible copy and its SEO representation must agree.

The November 24 instruction governs provider-name display across customer-facing surfaces. Earlier About/testimonial exceptions must not be used to reintroduce named-provider marketing. Preserve genuine reviews and their original wording as evidence; do not fabricate or silently rewrite a customer quotation. If a quote contains a provider name, omit that quote from authored promotional copy or remove its public display as a whole instead of altering the quote. Customer attribution and calendar month names are not provider-name mentions.

## Booking presentation

Use the supported Square setting **Remove staff from the online booking site = ON** to hide public staff mentions. Preserve real team records, internal identities, service assignments, prices, duration, hours, payroll, permissions and existing appointments. Do not rename or delete staff to solve a display problem. Independently verify public service details, appointment summary and availability after the setting is saved.

The online-booking setting does not by itself prove that confirmation emails, SMS messages, receipts or calendar attachments omit names. Inspect available template previews separately; do not send communications or create real appointments for QA without the corresponding authorization.

Official setting: https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/5351-manage-your-square-appointments-account-settings

## Verification and continuity

Before release, check visible and collapsed content, booking labels, captions/alt text, page-code SEO markup, metadata, social fields and JSON-LD. Correct actual current source occurrences, then verify the customer-facing result. Do not rebuild or republish already-neutral pages merely to show activity.

Historical receipts and rollback snapshots remain historical evidence. This current standard governs new/edited copy and overrides older copy decisions; it does not authorize speculative restoration, unrelated sitewide publication, altered quotations, customer-data changes or operational staff changes. The latest page-specific checkpoint still supplies the technical baseline and release state.
