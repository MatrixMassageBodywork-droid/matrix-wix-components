# Website provider-name standard

Effective 2026-11-07. Owner instruction: remove provider names from websites and do not use them in future website content.

Exception: the owner explicitly instructed that the About page must remain unchanged. Preserve its provider references.

Applies to every other Matrix website page, landing page, offer, blog post, FAQ, image alternative text/caption, title, meta/social description, and structured data. Use Matrix, the studio, your massage therapist, your skincare professional, or your lash technician as appropriate. Do not identify staff by first or full name, or add provider biographies/name-based headings. Describe services and qualifications only when verified.

Preserve genuine customer review wording using an accurate excerpt that omits the provider name, or an explicitly marked replacement such as [therapist]; never invent a review. Customer attribution is not a provider name.

Use the current studio booking destination rather than legacy person-named booking links. Do not change operational staff identities, booking assignments, credentials, account owners, internal documentation or archived rollback evidence as part of website copy cleanup.

QA before each website release: inspect visible content, collapsed FAQ answers, page-code SEO markup, metadata, social fields and JSON-LD for provider names. Fix the source and verify the public URL. This rule supersedes older approved website copy that included staff names.
