Why this approach

Why a four-week skin starting plan?
In a randomized trial of 95 women, a 660 nm red-light mask used two or three times weekly for four weeks improved some measured wrinkle lengths and appearance satisfaction. The study did not find a difference on every wrinkle measure or an advantage of three sessions over two. It supports a consistent skin routine; Matrix uses different equipment, so the study’s exposure dose is not copied.
Read the research: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40167796/

Build on your visits

You chose nutrition support, so here are practical ways to build on your visits. Start with one or two changes you can repeat this week.

Pair protein with color
Try Greek yogurt or fortified soy yogurt with berries at breakfast; at lunch, pair eggs, chicken, tofu or lentils with bell peppers or broccoli. Protein provides tissue building blocks, while vitamin C is needed for normal collagen formation. Make this a daily habit alongside your skin or comfort routine.
NIH: vitamin C and collagen: https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminC-HealthProfessional/

A practical skin-support plate
Build dinner around fish, tofu or beans, colorful vegetables and olive oil, with nuts or seeds if they suit your diet. This gives your routine variety, protein and unsaturated fats without relying on a single “beauty” ingredient.
Mediterranean-pattern nutrition research: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27440261/

A skin-specific option: oral hyaluronic acid
For fine lines and hydration, oral hyaluronic acid is an option to discuss. A small, industry-affiliated randomized trial in 40 adults found improvements in hydration and wrinkle measures after 12 weeks using 120 mg daily. This is a studied option to consider alongside your routine, not a guaranteed result or a personal dose instruction. Choose a product only after checking its ingredients, suitability and label with your clinician or pharmacist.
Oral hyaluronic acid skin trial: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34203487/
