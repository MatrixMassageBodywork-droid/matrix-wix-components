# Evidence used for R11
Verified 2026-11-09 UTC. Client copy distinguishes practical Matrix schedule from studied devices/doses. Study summaries are not promises of Matrix outcomes.

- Skin RCT: 95 women; 660 nm mask 2 or 3 times/week, 4 weeks; selected wrinkle measures and satisfaction improved; no advantage for 3 over 2 and not every scale improved. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40167796/
- Hair RCT: laser comb 3 times/week for 26 weeks; increased terminal hair density in pattern loss. Four weeks is not a regrowth deadline. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24474647/
- Knee pilot: 31 patients; individualized 850 nm twice/week for 5 weeks; pain improvement vs sham. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36286574/
- Knee review, very low certainty: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38775202/
- Muscle soreness review: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40954632/
- Whole-body review: 5 studies/105 participants, some sleep findings; no established exercise performance/fatigue benefit. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39883205/
- Wound evidence is medical, not a spa protocol: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13279567/
- Thyroid feasibility study: specialized targeted laser plus supplements; no automated thyroid exposure/supplement stack. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10455109/
- Vitamin C and normal collagen: https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminC-HealthProfessional/
- Mediterranean dietary pattern: long-term inflammatory biomarkers in high-CVD-risk adults, not a skin-treatment trial. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27440261/
- Protein/resistance training: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28698222/
- Oral hyaluronic acid: small industry-affiliated RCT, 40 adults, 120 mg/day 12 weeks, skin hydration/wrinkle outcomes. Offered as an optional discussion, not an automatically prescribed product/dose. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34203487/
- Curcumin: open-label knee OA trial, formulation-specific. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30975196/ Safety context: https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/turmeric
- Magnesium sleep RCT: 155 adults, 250 mg elemental/day, modest self-reported benefit at 4 weeks. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40918053/ Food/interactions: https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Magnesium-HealthProfessional/
- AAD hair nutrition: https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/hair-loss/treatment/diagnosis-treat/ and https://www.aad.org/news/supplements-debunking-common-myths
- Collagen supplements not promoted as proven: 2025 review found no benefit in high-quality/nonindustry-funded subgroups. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40324552/
