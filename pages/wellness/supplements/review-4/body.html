LOCAL SUPPLEMENT SHOPPING • YOUR QUESTIONS

Supplements & vitamins:
Salem-area questions answered.

Where can I find health supplements and vitamins in Salem, WI?

Matrix Massage & Bodywork offers access to vitamins and health supplements through its linked Fullscript store. Our studio is at 24804 75th St, Salem, WI 53168. Browse the existing plans online; monthly Matrix preorder bundles are coming soon.

Can I order from Paddock Lake, Twin Lakes, Burlington, Union Grove or Bristol?

Yes. You can explore the Matrix Fullscript store online from these communities without booking an in-studio appointment. Available products, prices and delivery options are shown in Fullscript. Matrix has one studio in Salem, not a separate location in each town.

Are Matrix preorders and Fullscript orders the same?

No. Fullscript purchases are handled through Fullscript. The upcoming monthly Matrix bundles will be a separate preorder offering paid through Square. Monthly Matrix preorders are not open yet.

Can I pick up vitamins or supplements at the Salem studio?

The future Matrix preorder offers will explain whether pickup or shipping is available. Pickup is not currently promised through this page. Use the linked Fullscript store for current ordering, and text 262-939-9325 before visiting the studio for a supplement pickup.

When will Matrix place its monthly supplement orders?

The proposed schedule is twice monthly, on the 1st and 15th. Each released offer will show its exact request deadline, payment deadline and fulfillment information before you order. Submitting a request does not establish payment; only verified full payment by the displayed cutoff qualifies for that purchasing cycle.

Do I need every product in a Fullscript supplement plan?

Review the products and available purchase options in the linked Fullscript plan. A collection is not a personalized determination that every listed product is appropriate for you. Ask your healthcare professional which, if any, products fit your needs.

How do I choose supplements that are appropriate for me?

Review the product label and discuss suitability with a qualified healthcare professional, particularly if you take medication, are pregnant or breastfeeding, have a medical condition or are preparing for surgery. This page does not diagnose or treat a condition.

Will requesting a bundle sign me up for weekly marketing emails?

No. The proposed preorder form keeps required order communications separate from an optional, unchecked subscription to weekly offers. Marketing subscriptions will include an unsubscribe option.
