## Clinical source verification
- Bragato et al., 2025, randomized sham-controlled trial, 95 women; 660nm mask 2 or 3 times/week for 4 weeks. Selected wrinkle-length and satisfaction outcomes improved; not all measures improved; no superiority of three visits established. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40167796/
- Pinto et al., 2022, randomized double-blind pilot, 31 knee-OA patients; individualized 850nm protocol twice/week for 5 weeks. Lower pain scores versus sham at selected sessions and follow-up. Not evidence of identical full-body BioMax dose. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36286574/
- American Academy of Dermatology overview supports education on red/near-infrared treatment and skin concerns, with individualized precautions. https://www.aad.org/public/cosmetic/safety/red-light-therapy
- A 2–3/week four-week starting rhythm is identified as a Matrix planning approach for suitable goals, not a universal evidence-based medical prescription. Actual exposure/device settings are confirmed during intake.
