RED LIGHT THERAPY QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

What is red light therapy?

Red light therapy, also called photobiomodulation, uses red and near-infrared wavelengths of light to support your body's natural cellular energy. Guests use it for recovery, skin glow, relaxation and everyday wellness.

​

What equipment does Matrix use?

Matrix uses professional-grade PlatinumLED BioMax 900 red and near-infrared panels in a private, comfortable room.

Which red light therapy option can I book?

Full body red light therapy is $25.99. Face and head is $14.99, including as a massage add-on. Full body during an existing session is a $22.99 enhancement, added to your main service price. Explore your options with our red light quiz.

​

How often should I use red light therapy?

Consistency is where red light shines. Many guests start with two to three sessions a week, then settle into a regular maintenance rhythm. We will help you build a plan that fits your goals and schedule.

​

What does a red light session feel like?

Relaxing. You rest comfortably in a private room while the panels deliver gentle warmth and light. There is no downtime, so you can head right back into your day.

​

Can I pair red light with other services?

Yes. Red light pairs beautifully with massage, lymphatic drainage, facials, Japanese Head Spa, grounding and infrared sauna for a complete recovery visit.

​

What is your cancellation policy?

Your appointment time is reserved just for you, so we ask for at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Cancellations made within 24 hours of your appointment, for any reason, and no-shows are charged the full price of the service plus an automatic 20% gratuity.

​

Do I need a card on file to book?

Yes. To secure your appointment, complete our intake form and credit card authorization form when you book. If you have a Matrix gift card on file instead, its remaining balance is applied toward any late-cancellation fee.
