BEFORE YOU VISIT

A few helpful details.

Can I combine a facial and Head Spa?

Yes. The Matrix Head Spa + Hydrating Glow Facial combines scalp care with a mini facial and facial massage. The current menu also includes men's scalp-and-facial combinations. Check the booking menu for the experience and appointment length you prefer.

Do you offer lash fills as well as full sets?

Yes. Classic, Hybrid, and Volume lash extensions each have full-set and one-, two-, and three-week fill appointments. Choose the matching style and timing in the current booking menu.

Is hair drying included with Head Spa?

Head Spa services leave hair wet. Luxury Blowout Styling is a separate add-on appointment with a cosmetologist, when available. Reserve it with your visit if you would like your hair professionally dried and styled.

Where do I go, and how do I book?

Visit Matrix Massage & Bodywork at 24804 75th St, Salem, WI 53168. Book online through our current Square menu or text 262-939-9325. Our Salem studio welcomes guests from Salem Lakes, Paddock Lake, Bristol, Twin Lakes, Kenosha, and nearby Antioch, Illinois.
