How much do lash extensions cost at Matrix?

Classic full sets are $170, Hybrid full sets are $200 and Volume full sets are $220. One-, two- and three-week fills range from $55 to $95, depending on style and timing. See the pricing table above and confirm the current service price in the booking menu.

Should I choose Classic, Hybrid or Volume lashes?

Classic gives a softly defined, natural-looking finish. Hybrid adds texture and a fuller look. Volume gives fuller definition and a more dramatic finish. Tell your lash technician how subtle or bold you want your lashes to look so you can discuss a style that works with your natural lashes.

Can I ask questions before booking?

Yes. Text Matrix at 262-939-9325 with questions about lash styles, full sets or fills. We can help you choose an appointment before you book. Current availability is shown in the online booking menu.

How long does a lash appointment take?

The current booking menu lists full sets at 60 minutes and fill appointments at 45 minutes. Check the appointment details when reserving. If you already have extensions or are unsure which service to select, contact us before booking.

How often should I book a lash fill?

Matrix offers one-, two- and three-week fills for Classic, Hybrid and Volume lashes. The right interval depends on your natural lash shedding, how many extensions remain and the look you want to maintain. Ask your lash technician which appointment fits your lashes.

Can I book a fill if another salon applied my lashes?

Please text Matrix before booking. Let us know your current lash style, when it was applied and how your lashes look now. We can help you choose an appointment after reviewing what is needed; a fill may not be the right service for every existing set.

What should I do before my first lash appointment?

Arrive with clean lashes and no mascara or eye makeup. Tell us about previous adhesive reactions or eye-area sensitivity before your visit. If you have an eye infection or inflamed skin around your eyes, wait until it has healed and contact the studio before booking.

How do I care for eyelash extensions?

Follow the aftercare directions provided for your set, including cleansing, brushing and guidance about water, steam and eye products. Avoid rubbing, pulling or picking at extensions. Ask before using a new lash serum, oil or makeup remover with your set.

Are lash extensions the same as a lash lift?

No. Extensions add fibers to your natural lashes for a chosen length and fullness; a lash lift changes the curl of your existing lashes. This page is for Matrix Classic, Hybrid and Volume lash extensions and their maintenance fills.

Can I get a natural-looking set for everyday wear?

Yes—Classic is our softly defined option. Share the look you want, whether that is a subtle everyday finish, more texture or a fuller lash line. Your natural lashes and chosen style affect the finished result.
