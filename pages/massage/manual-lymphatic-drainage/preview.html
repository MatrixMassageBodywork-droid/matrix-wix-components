Understand the approach

What is manual
lymphatic drainage?

The lymphatic system helps transport excess fluid from body tissues. Manual lymphatic drainage is a gentle hands-on method that uses a specific sequence of light movements rather than strong muscle pressure.

People sometimes associate this approach with swelling care or post-treatment support. Its role depends on the underlying condition, and it should not replace medical assessment, compression therapy or another treatment plan recommended by your healthcare professional.

MLD is not a general “detox” or immune-boosting treatment. We focus on explaining what the method involves and when appropriate guidance is needed.

Gentle pressure, thoughtful screening

How does MLD differ
from a traditional massage?

01

Lighter touch

The technique uses light, rhythmic pressure. More pressure is not automatically better and is not how MLD is designed to work.

02

An individual plan

The choice of areas and sequence depends on your goals and relevant health information. Tell your provider about recent surgery, lymph node procedures, active illness or unexplained swelling.

03

A different objective

Deep tissue techniques primarily focus on muscles and tension, while MLD is a specialized method involving fluid movement in lymphatic pathways.

Before your appointment

When to ask your
healthcare professional first.

If you are recovering from surgery, receiving cancer treatment, have had lymph nodes removed, or are managing swelling from a medical condition, contact your treating clinician and share their guidance with the massage provider before scheduling. Some situations require a specialized lymphedema service rather than a general wellness appointment.

Do not book MLD without appropriate medical guidance if you have unexplained or new swelling, a suspected blood clot, fever or active infection, significant heart or kidney concerns, or another condition that could make fluid movement or massage unsafe. Seek medical evaluation for urgent or unexplained symptoms.

This service does not diagnose or treat medical conditions, and no immediate reduction in swelling or number of required sessions can be guaranteed.

Is lymphatic drainage massage painful?

It is generally performed with gentle, rhythmic movements rather than deep pressure. Tell your provider if anything feels uncomfortable.

Is manual lymphatic drainage the same as deep tissue massage?

No. The techniques differ in pressure and purpose. Deep tissue massage uses more focused muscle work; MLD uses light movement in a specific sequence.

Is MLD a detox massage?

It should not be marketed as a proven general detoxification treatment. Your liver, kidneys and other organs handle normal waste processing; MLD has more specific clinical uses related to fluid management.

Can I schedule after surgery?

Ask your surgeon or treating healthcare professional first. Timing and suitability depend on the procedure, current healing and medical guidance.

What if my swelling has no clear cause?

New, persistent or unexplained swelling should be medically evaluated before booking a massage.

How often do people need MLD?

There is no universal schedule. Any treatment frequency for medical swelling should follow an individualized healthcare plan.
