FAQ's

Q: What is deep tissue massage?
A: Deep tissue massage is a focused technique that targets deeper layers of muscle and fascia to help relieve chronic tension, improve mobility, and support recovery from physical strain.

 

Q: Will deep tissue massage hurt?
A: You may feel firm pressure or mild discomfort in tight areas, but it should never be painful. Your therapist will check in and adjust pressure to your comfort level throughout the session.

 

Q: Is deep tissue massage good for everyone?
A: Deep tissue is great for athletes, active individuals, and those with chronic muscle tension. If you have certain health conditions, we’ll review your intake form and discuss the best approach for your body.

 

Q: How should I prepare for my session?
A: Wear comfortable clothes and drink plenty of water before and after your massage. You’ll also receive a complimentary glass of hydrogen water post-session to support recovery and hydration.
