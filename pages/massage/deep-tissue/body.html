What is deep
tissue massage?

Deep tissue massage uses slower, more focused methods to work with muscles and surrounding soft tissue that feel persistently tight. Your therapist may spend extra time around your shoulders, neck, back or legs based on your needs.

At Matrix, your session is tailored to your comfort, your areas of focus and the appointment you choose. You can ask for full-body work or spend more time on a specific area.

Does this sound familiar?

  • Your neck and shoulders feel tight after long hours at a desk.
  • Your back or legs feel overworked from an active routine.
  • You want focused attention, with pressure you can comfortably settle into.

Does deep tissue massage have to hurt?

No. More pressure is not always better. Your comfort matters, and you can request less or more pressure within the appropriate treatment approach.

Is deep tissue the same as Swedish massage?

Swedish massage usually emphasizes longer, flowing movements and relaxation, while deep tissue uses slower, more focused work. Techniques can overlap depending on your goals. Explore Swedish massage.

Can I focus on my upper back and shoulders?

Yes. Let your therapist know where you would like the time spent. The plan can be adjusted within the appointment you booked.

What if I have an injury or medical condition?

Tell your provider about it before your session. Some situations require a modified approach or advice from your healthcare professional before massage. Massage should not delay medical care for a new or unexplained problem.

How should I prepare for my session?

Complete your intake information and arrive ready to discuss your goals, health history and preferred areas of focus. Wear comfortable clothes and let us know about any changes since your last visit.
