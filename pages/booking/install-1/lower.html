BEFORE YOU BOOK

A few quick answers.

Need a hand? Text 262-939-9325.

How do I book an appointment?

Choose a service in the Square scheduler, select an available appointment and follow the booking steps. Review the service details and booking terms before confirming.

What if the booking menu does not load?

Use Open Full Booking Menu to open the same scheduler directly. You can also text 262-939-9325 for help choosing a service.

Which service should I choose?

Explore our massage, facial, Japanese Head Spa, lash, waxing and wellness pages for treatment details. If you are unsure which option fits, text us before booking.

Who qualifies for the introductory facial offer?

The introductory facial is for guests new to Matrix facial services, including existing massage, Head Spa and wellness guests who have not had a facial here. Read the full offer terms before booking.

How do I receive the $85 facial follow-up?

Reserve the recommended 60-minute Custom Facial follow-up before leaving your introductory appointment. It is arranged at checkout, subject to availability, and cannot be combined with other promotions.

Where is my appointment?

All appointments are at Matrix Massage & Bodywork, 24804 75th St, Salem, WI 53168. We welcome guests from Salem Lakes, Paddock Lake, Bristol, Twin Lakes, Kenosha, Union Grove and nearby Antioch.

How do I change an appointment?

Check your Square confirmation for appointment-management options and the booking terms. If you need assistance, text 262-939-9325. Cancellation and rescheduling policies shown during booking apply.

Can I buy a gift card instead?

Yes. Visit our gift card page to purchase an eGift card. Buying a gift card does not reserve an appointment; the recipient can book a service separately.
