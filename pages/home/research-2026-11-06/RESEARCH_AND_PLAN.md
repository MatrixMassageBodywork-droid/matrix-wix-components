Research date: November 6, 2026 (America/Chicago)
Project: FACIAL WEB SEO PW
Status: RESEARCHED / BASELINE VERIFIED / RECOMMENDATIONS PROPOSED.
Website changes, draft installation, new pages, promotions and publication: NONE.

## Recommendation
Use the homepage as the overview of the entire Matrix experience, with therapeutic massage remaining prominent. Give each major service a clear photo, a short useful description and a direct link to its established service page. Feature a compact Current Specials section and create one permanent Specials hub only after approval. Keep the existing individual facial offer page and the published Gift Cards page.

The opportunity is both discovery and conversion: people already arrive at the homepage, but its current body does not make the full service range equally easy to understand or explore. A broad service overview can help visitors choose their next step and improve internal linking without sacrificing the homepage's established massage relevance.

## Current homepage audit
Fresh public HTTP read: 200 response; one H1; self-referencing canonical at https://www.matrixmassage.net/; no robots noindex meta observed. This is a markup check, not Google URL Inspection or a speed/mobile rendering test.

Native Wix page ID: ghmgt. Existing title: Japanese Head Spa, Facials & Massage in Salem, WI | Matrix.
The current title and H1 already cover the broader offering. Preserve both for the initial layout redesign; a title experiment should be separate and evidence-led.

Observed strengths:
- An established homepage with meaningful organic search traffic.
- Existing links to massage, facial, head spa and wellness pages.
- Existing location/contact information and Square booking path.
- Existing FAQPage, LocalBusiness and WebSite structured data in the public response.

Observed gaps:
- Most main-body content and repeated booking messages remain massage focused.
- Services are largely named in paragraphs rather than presented as an organized, visual menu.
- The current facial offer is buried in an FAQ instead of an actionable offer card.
- Lash Extensions is present in the current navigation but lacks a clear homepage-body introduction.
- The current menu contains both Japanese Head Spa and Head Spa pointing to the same page.
- The 'free assessment' section links to the massage service hub. Its label should be reconciled with what the visitor actually receives before redesign.
- Existing homepage copy makes broad statements about hormones, metabolism, energy and healing. Replace vague promises with concise service descriptions and realistic customer goals during the approved copy stage.
- There is no Specials hub in the 33-URL static-page sitemap. An existing individual facial offer route is present; this is not a reason to recreate it.
- Current native FAQ policy wording includes a cancellation gratuity provision. Do not silently change business policy as part of homepage design; check it against the approved current policy before reusing it.

## Specials: one permanent page plus homepage summaries
Proposed navigation label: Specials.
Proposed permanent path: /specials.
Proposed search title: Massage & Spa Specials in Salem, WI | Matrix.
These are proposals, not saved site settings. Confirm the complete native/dynamic route inventory and redirects before creating the page.

The Specials page should contain:
- New-guest offers, seasonal offers and verified packages/series, grouped only when there is content to justify it.
- The real selling price, duration, genuine comparison price, dollar savings, eligibility, redemption instructions, expiry and exclusions.
- Links to relevant service pages and the exact valid booking/purchase route.
- Current information when an offer ends. Hide expired homepage cards; mark old individual offers ended and point to active alternatives. Keep the hub's permanent URL.
- No fake urgency, continuously resetting countdowns, invented savings or unpublished seasonal promotions.

Use the same offer data for the homepage teaser and full offer page so price and terms cannot drift. Future implementation should support start/end dates in America/Chicago, display priority, approved/active status and an automatic or reliably managed expiry rule. This is a specification, not an automation created by this audit.

Illustrative card grounded in current published copy:
New to Matrix facials — 45 minutes — $79 — Save $16 against the advertised regular 45-minute price of $95.
Eligibility includes clients who already receive other Matrix services but are new to facials.
The $85 60-minute follow-up is conditional on reserving it before leaving the first appointment; it is not a second unrestricted online introductory offer.

Before publishing a savings badge, verify the same-service comparison in Square. A saved offer source lists the follow-up's regular price as $100, while other project service pricing has used $110 for a 60-minute Custom Facial. Do not advertise follow-up dollar savings until that discrepancy is resolved. The approved follow-up sale price remains $85.

Do not invent a lash promotion from older campaign discussions. The homepage may prioritize an active, verified lash offer when one exists. Keep gift cards visible even when no gift-card promotion is active.

## SEO preservation and measurement
- Preserve the homepage URL, page identity, canonical, current title/H1 and established massage relevance for the first design release.
- Maintain meaningful massage content and links to deep tissue, Swedish, MLD and the prenatal option on the massage hub. Do not remove topics solely to make room for new cards.
- Keep crawlable HTML links with meaningful labels. Google explicitly recommends real anchor links and descriptive text.
- Preserve and reconcile existing LocalBusiness, WebSite and FAQ markup; do not automatically add duplicate schemas or ratings. FAQ markup is not a promise of rich results or AI citations.
- Use short distinct summaries on the homepage; keep detailed service targeting on established service pages.
- Avoid city-list stuffing, fabricated local branches and duplicated city landing pages.
- Optimize photography, retain accessible text/contrast and ensure the mobile booking action is easy to reach.
- Measure service-card clicks, offer views and booking handoffs by originating section. A Square booking click is not a completed appointment.
- Review Search Console and conversion data after roughly 28 days and again after 8–12 weeks, allowing for seasonality and the earlier service-page releases. A review schedule is proposed only; no automation was created.

The next authorized deliverable should be a complete desktop/mobile homepage design proof plus a Specials-page plan, with exact service copy and verified offer terms. No live installation or publication is authorized by this research request.

3. Preserve the header/footer, About page and testimonials; identify any separately approved navigation change.
4. Verify the current Square service/offer facts and all button destinations.
5. Build the full proof, with no public website mutations.
6. Test mobile/desktop layout, one visible H1, image loading, keyboard access, links, offer expiry/eligibility and visible FAQ/schema agreement.
7. Present the complete proof for design approval. Install and publish only within the subsequent explicit authorization.

NOT TESTED in this audit: homepage visual browser rendering, Core Web Vitals, physical-device mobile behavior, conversion tracking accuracy, completed booking attribution, local Maps rank grid, and live Square promotion redemption. No claim is made that the current site has failed any of these tests.
