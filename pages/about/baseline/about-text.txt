Hello
Licensed Massage Therapist | Red Light Therapy & Holistic Wellness
Matrix Massage & Bodywork | Salem, WI
Hi, I’m DaNelle — a Licensed Massage Therapist & wellness practitioner specializing in therapeutic massage and red light therapy in Salem, Wisconsin. At Matrix Massage & Bodywork, I help people find real, lasting relief from pain, stress, fatigue, and tension through an integrative blend of massage therapy, bodywork, and advanced wellness treatments.
My Story
Before I stepped into the world of wellness, I spent over 15 years in the corporate world — first as a corporate recruiter, then running my own headhunting firm for 10 years. I was successful on paper, but something was shifting. When the world changed during COVID, so did I...
​
I started noticing the toll stress, sitting, and high-performance pressure were taking on my body — tension, fatigue, aches, burnout. And I realized: I didn’t feel at home in my body anymore. I had to do something I was passionate about & my passion for the corporate/recruitment world was gone.
​
That moment became the catalyst for everything that followed. I knew I needed to change my life before it was too late. So I took a leap — and began my journey into fitness, holistic health, nervous system healing, and therapeutic bodywork. And I haven’t looked back.
​
I combine clinical training with ongoing research into functional wellness, nervous system support, and non-invasive healing therapies. I’ve built Matrix Massage to be a space that’s calm, clean, results-driven, and client-centered.
​
I'm not just here to give you a massage — I'm here to help you feel like yourself again.
​
Why I Do This Work
Matrix Massage & Bodywork was born from a personal transformation — and now it exists to help others through theirs. My mission is to help you feel good in your body again. Not just for an hour, but for the long haul.
Whether you're seeking massage therapy in Salem WI or want to explore red light therapy for healing and recovery, my work blends modern tools with intuitive, client-centered care.
​
What I Offer
At my studio, you’ll find more than just massage. I offer a full range of wellness services to support pain relief, muscle recovery, nervous system balance, and overall well-being:
​
Red Light Therapy using top of the line medical grade, FDA approved Red Light & NIR
Deep Tissue, Swedish & Lymphatic Massage
Cupping & Gua Sha to release tension and improve circulation
Hydrogen Water & hot towels with EVERY session FREE
Aromatherapy & Magnesium add ons for full-body support
Each session is customized to what your body truly needs — no cookie-cutter routines, no rushed appointments.
​
Who I Work With
I specialize in helping people who are dealing with:
​
Chronic pain or tightness in the neck, shoulders, or back
Fitness enthusiasts and semi-pro athletes looking to improve recovery, prevent injury, and support peak performance
Fatigue, stress, or adrenal burnout
Post-injury or post-surgical recovery
Lymphatic and immune system support
Muscle soreness, poor circulation, or mobility challenges
Many of my clients are professionals, athletes, caregivers, and everyday people just trying to feel better and function better — without drugs or invasive treatments.
