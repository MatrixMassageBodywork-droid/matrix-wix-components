THE PERSON BEHIND MATRIX

Hi, I’m DaNelle.

I believe good care starts with being heard.

I’m a Wisconsin licensed massage therapist and the owner of Matrix Massage & Bodywork in Salem. I created Matrix to be a place where you can slow down, feel comfortable, and receive care shaped around what matters to you.

My work centers on therapeutic massage and bodywork. I take time to understand where you hold tension, how your daily routine affects you, and what you want from your visit. Your preferences, comfort, and goals guide the session.

Today, that same personal attention extends throughout the studio—from massage and recovery-focused wellness to facials, Japanese Head Spa, and beauty services. Matrix has grown, and the purpose is still personal: helping you make room to care for yourself.

HOW I GOT HERE

A different kind
of meaningful work.

Before Matrix, I spent more than 15 years in the corporate world, first as a recruiter and then running my own headhunting firm for a decade. I knew the long hours, the time at a desk, and the pressure of always being “on.”

During the pandemic, I began to recognize how much stress, tension, and burnout were affecting my own well-being. My priorities changed. A growing interest in fitness, wellness, and therapeutic bodywork led me toward work that felt more connected to the way I wanted to live.

That experience still shapes how I care for people. I understand how easy it is to put yourself last—and how meaningful it can be to finally make time for yourself.

Who owns Matrix Massage & Bodywork?

Matrix Massage & Bodywork is independently owned by DaNelle Borglin, a Wisconsin licensed massage therapist. The studio is located in Salem, Wisconsin.

What brought DaNelle into massage therapy?

After more than 15 years in the corporate world, including a decade running her own headhunting firm, DaNelle began pursuing a different path during the pandemic. Her experience with stress, tension, and burnout led her toward fitness, wellness, and therapeutic bodywork. That experience continues to inform her personal approach to care.

What services does the studio offer today?

Matrix offers therapeutic massage and bodywork, custom facials, Japanese Head Spa, lash extensions, face and body waxing, red light therapy, and infrared sauna. Explore the service pages for details and the current booking menu for available appointments.

What should I expect at my first visit?

Your visit begins with a conversation about your goals, preferences, and relevant health or skin considerations for the service you chose. Ask questions and let your provider know what feels comfortable. If you are unsure which service to book, text 262-939-9325 for help choosing a starting point.

Where is Matrix, and which communities does it serve?

Matrix Massage & Bodywork is at 24804 75th St, Salem, WI 53168. The studio welcomes guests from Salem Lakes, Paddock Lake, Bristol, Pleasant Prairie, Twin Lakes, Trevor, Kenosha, Burlington, Union Grove, Genoa City, and Antioch, Illinois. All appointments are provided at this one Salem location.

How do I book an appointment?

Use the online booking menu to choose a service and view current availability, or text 262-939-9325. Online scheduling is handled through Square Appointments.

Where can I follow Matrix online?
