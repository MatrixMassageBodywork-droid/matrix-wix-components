## What saving meant, and why production could proceed

The approved SEO edits had already been saved and read back successfully. The remaining proposal was an additional Deep Tissue schema workaround prompted by a difference between the test page’s server response and its rendered browser output. Leaving that proposed workaround unapplied would have left a question about the consistency of that page’s extra structured information. The available evidence did not establish an outage, loss of ordinary search eligibility or a reason to delay every page.

Google’s minimum technical requirements concern crawler access, a working HTTP response and indexable content; structured data is not listed as a minimum requirement for inclusion. Meeting those requirements still does not guarantee indexing. Source: [Google Search technical requirements](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/technical).

The owner’s latest instruction directed that website production and SEO proceed today. Existing approval for A01–A08 was carried forward. Wix’s supported **Publish this version** operation released the already tested snapshot, version 1121. Its documented effect is to replace the live site and expose that version to all visitors. Source: [Wix: Publishing a Test Site](https://support.wix.com/en/article/publishing-a-test-site).

This report records the completed release. It supersedes the previous report’s current publication hold and its untested status for Swedish, Manual Lymphatic Drainage and Pleasant Prairie. Historical observations and proposals remain available as history.
