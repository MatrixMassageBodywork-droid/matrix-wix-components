Is sauna a detox treatment?
Matrix offers infrared sauna for general wellness and relaxation. We do not present sweating or sauna use as a medical detox treatment.
Who should avoid sauna?
Heat exposure is not appropriate for everyone. If you are pregnant, have cardiovascular concerns, take medications that affect heat tolerance or have another medical concern, ask your healthcare professional before sauna use.
